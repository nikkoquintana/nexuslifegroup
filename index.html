An agency built on saying the quiet part out loud.

Most people buy life insurance once, from whoever called first, and never look at it again. We think that's the wrong way to protect a family.

Nexus Life Group is an independent agency licensed in all 50 states, with our main office in Salt Lake City, Utah. We're not owned by a carrier and we're not paid to push one product. Our job is to understand what you need the policy to do, shop it across every carrier we represent, and show you the two or three that fit best, side by side.

We tell you the premium before you apply. We set up your draft date so the first payment lands when it works for you. And after the policy is issued, you keep our number, not a call center's.

Four kinds of coverage. One honest conversation about which fits.

Every policy we write falls into one of these. On the call we'll narrow it to the right one for your situation and show you what it costs from the carriers that rate you best.

Term lifeCoverage for a set number of years

The most affordable way to cover a large amount for the years it matters most: while the kids are home, while the mortgage is open, while your income is what the household runs on. Terms of 10 to 30 years, often approved the same day with no exam.

Built forParents, homeowners, anyone replacing an income

Whole lifePermanent coverage, level premium

A death benefit that never expires and a premium that never rises, with cash value that builds inside the policy. It costs more than term for the same amount, but it's predictable for life and works well when the goal is a guaranteed legacy.

Built forLifetime protection, guaranteed premiums, cash value

Final expenseSimplified whole life, ages 50 to 85

A smaller policy, typically $5,000 to $50,000, designed to cover funeral costs, medical bills, and debts so your family isn't handed the invoice. Health questions only, no exam, and many plans accept ongoing conditions.

Built forCovering end-of-life costs without burdening family

Indexed universal lifePermanent coverage tied to a market index

Protection plus a cash value account credited on an index like the S&P 500, with a cap on the upside and a floor against losses. Flexible premiums, and cash value you can access later for retirement income or emergencies.

Built forLong-term savers, supplemental retirement income
