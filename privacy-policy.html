Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Nexus Life Group LLC ("Nexus Life Group," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or request a quote, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, state of residence, age, the type of coverage you are interested in, and anything you write in the message field. If you apply for a policy, we collect the additional information the insurance carrier requires to underwrite it, which may include health, financial, and beneficiary information.

Our website may also collect standard technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, and referring website, through cookies or similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your quote request and answer your questions
  • To compare and recommend life insurance policies suited to your situation
  • To submit applications to insurance carriers on your behalf, with your authorization
  • To service your policy after it is issued, including beneficiary changes and claims support
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website
  • To comply with insurance licensing, record-keeping, and other legal obligations

Consent to be contacted

By submitting a form on this website, you consent to receive calls, text messages, and emails from Nexus Life Group and its licensed agents at the phone number and email address you provide, including through automated dialing systems and prerecorded messages, for the purpose of discussing your quote and related insurance products. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and out of calls or emails by contacting us using the details below.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to serve you:

  • With insurance carriers and their underwriters when you apply for coverage
  • With service providers that help us operate, such as our customer relationship management, phone, email, and website hosting platforms, who are bound to use it only on our behalf
  • With regulators, courts, or law enforcement when required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • In connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of our business, subject to this policy

Health and financial information

Information you provide during an insurance application is handled in accordance with applicable state insurance privacy laws and the federal Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act. We use it solely to obtain and service coverage and share it only with the carriers and parties involved in that process.

Data retention and security

We keep your information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet insurance record-keeping requirements, which are typically several years after a policy ends. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • You can opt out of marketing calls, texts, and emails at any time.
  • You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though some site features may not work as intended.

Depending on your state, you may have additional rights, including the right to know what information we have collected and to request its deletion. To exercise any of these rights, contact us below.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will reflect the most recent version.
